FXNavigator Account Deletion Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Important: Account deletion requests cannot be submitted through this website. All account deletion actions must be initiated directly from within the FXNavigator mobile application.

How to Delete Your Account

FXNavigator provides users with full control over their accounts and personal information. If you wish to permanently delete your account, please follow these steps:

  1. Open the FXNavigator application.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Navigate to Settings.
  4. Select Account & Privacy.
  5. Tap Delete Account.
  6. Read the confirmation message carefully.
  7. Confirm the deletion request.

Once confirmed, your account deletion request will be processed automatically.

What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted?

Deleting your account is a permanent action. Once completed, access to your account and associated data may no longer be available.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User profile information.
  • Email address associated with the account.
  • Notification preferences.
  • Saved settings and application preferences.
  • Watchlists and favorites.
  • Educational progress data.
  • Associated authentication records where applicable.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period when required for:

  • Security investigations.
  • Fraud prevention.
  • Legal compliance.
  • Regulatory obligations.
  • System integrity and auditing purposes.

Data Retention Period

Most personal account information is scheduled for deletion immediately after account deletion is confirmed. Certain system logs, security records, or legal compliance records may be retained for a limited period where required by applicable laws or regulations.

Third-Party Authentication Providers

If you signed in using:

  • Google
  • Yahoo
  • Apple
  • X (Twitter)

Deleting your FXNavigator account does not automatically delete your account with those providers. You may need to manage your connected applications directly from your provider's account settings.

Push Notifications

After account deletion is completed:

  • Session notifications will stop.
  • Economic calendar alerts will stop.
  • Market news notifications will stop.
  • Trading reminders will stop.

Subscription Information

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel subscriptions purchased through:

  • Google Play
  • Apple App Store

Users are responsible for managing and cancelling subscriptions through the platform where the subscription was purchased.

Warning: Account deletion is irreversible. Once completed, deleted account data may not be recoverable.

Need Help?

If you experience issues accessing your account or require assistance with the deletion process, please contact our support team.

Email: support@fxnavigator.app

Policy Updates

FXNavigator may update this Account Deletion Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
