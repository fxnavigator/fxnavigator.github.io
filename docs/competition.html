Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the competition.

When does the competition start? ▼
The competition runs for 30 days from the official start date announced in the app. Check the Competition section for the exact dates and live countdown.
Is the competition free to join? ▼
Yes, the competition is completely free. Simply download the FXNavigator app, create an account, and you are automatically eligible to participate.
How do I get my referral code? ▼
Open the FXNavigator app, go to the Competition section, and you will see your unique referral code. Tap to copy it and share with your friends.
How are points calculated? ▼
You earn 100 points per referral, 10 points for daily login, 5 points per day for login streaks, and 20 points for trading activity. Points are updated in real-time.
When and how are prizes paid? ▼
Prizes are paid within 7 business days after the competition ends. Winners will be contacted through the app with instructions for prize collection.
Can I participate from any country? ▼
Yes, the competition is open globally. However, prizes may be subject to local laws and tax regulations in your jurisdiction.
What happens if I violate the rules? ▼
Any violation of the anti-fraud policy, including fake referrals or multiple accounts, will result in immediate disqualification and permanent ban from the platform.
