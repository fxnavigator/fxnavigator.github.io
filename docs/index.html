Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about FXNavigator.

What is FXNavigator?
FXNavigator is a comprehensive Forex trading companion app available on iOS and Android. It provides live market sessions, an economic calendar, news, interactive education, an AI assistant and a directory of 32+ prop funding firms — all in one place.
Is FXNavigator free?
Yes. FXNavigator offers a free tier with full access to core features. An optional Premium subscription ($4.99/month) unlocks advanced features, removes ads and provides early access to new tools.
What platforms are supported?
FXNavigator is available on iOS and Android phones and tablets.
What languages are supported?
FXNavigator supports 12 languages: English, Arabic, French, Turkish, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Russian, Indonesian, Chinese, Japanese and Hindi.
What are prop firms?
Proprietary trading firms (prop firms) provide traders with capital to trade in exchange for a share of profits. Our directory lists 32+ firms with details on funding challenges, account sizes, platforms and special discounts.
How does the education system work?
FXNavigator includes 5 trading schools (ICT, SMC, Elliott Wave, SMT, VFX) with 8 progressive lessons each. Each lesson combines animated concept renderers, interactive chart exercises and quizzes. You earn XP, build streaks and unlock achievements as you progress.
Is FXNavigator financial advice?
No. FXNavigator is an educational and informational tool. It does not provide financial advice, investment recommendations or trading signals. Some directory links are affiliate links — we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Always verify details directly with the firm and consult a qualified financial advisor before trading.
